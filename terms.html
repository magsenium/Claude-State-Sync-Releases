What the extension is

A free, open-source developer tool that copies Claude Code's state — memory, skills, plans, CLAUDE.md and session transcripts — between computers you control, through your own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive account. The developer operates no service, stores none of your data, and has no access to your cloud storage.
